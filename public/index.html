FAQ

¶

Interested in contributing to, updating, or starting a new franchise order list?

¶

This is a serious commitment — we don't want half-finished pages live. Building a new list can easily take 10+ hours, and some, like Final Fantasy, have taken over 100. If you're still interested, or want to help with an existing list, head to that list's discussion page, or the organization discussion page if we don't have a list for it yet. The placeholders are just suggestions; we'd welcome others too.

Have fan art you want to share with our community?

¶

We're always looking for simple small original fan art to go alongside entries in the lists (with full in-place attribution), or for behind the cards on this page (footer attribution), and rotate through multiples when we can. Hand-drawn or digitally painted (Photoshop, tablet, etc.) work is welcome, but we'd prefer to avoid AI-generated art for now. Head to that list's discussion page, or the organization discussion page, to tell us about your work.

So we can display it freely, we only accept CC-BY licensed work. Let us know if something is being displayed in error.
